# Yuki
**What is Yuki?**

Yuki is a tiny, self‑contained intelligence designed to live entirely on the user’s device. It doesn’t rely on servers, data centers, or massive models. Instead, it grows from the knowledge the user gives it.

**Why does Yuki exist?**

Yuki's pure existence is to help you without the need for bigger servers and higher costs. It exists to prove that intelligence doesn’t need a data center to feel useful or alive. By staying small and local, Yuki gives users something modern AIs rarely offer: control. Everything it knows stays on their device.

**What are Yuki's features?**

* Yuki has built-in memory, allowing its knowledge to be updated across all sessions.
* Designed to be compact and efficient, Yuki responds quick as possible, even on low-end hardware.
* Yuki’s behavior evolves based on the knowledge it receives, allowing it to develop a tone or style that matches the user
  
## Requirements
* Python 3.10+
